I'm Abdul Malek, a Textile Engineering student at Jashore University of Science and Technology, Bangladesh. I work at the intersection of traditional textile science and modern software engineering — creating tools that make complex industry calculations accessible, intelligent, and sustainable.

My flagship project, TextileCalc v2.0, is a published open-source Python library with 60+ functions and AI-powered modules. I believe in zero-dependency design, deep domain knowledge, and making expert tools free for the global textile community.

Research paper

TextileCalc v2.0: A Comprehensive Open-Source Python Library for Textile Engineering Calculations

ResearchGate · 2024
Open Access
Python · Textile Engineering · AI

Published on ResearchGate, this paper documents the architecture, design philosophy, and technical implementation of TextileCalc v2.0 — the most comprehensive open-source Python library for textile engineering calculations. It covers the library's 13 modules, three novel AI-powered features, and demonstrates how zero-dependency pure Python can serve as a viable platform for production engineering tools.
